Scoring follows the official 3陸特 rules from 日本無線協会: each subject is 12 questions at 5 points (60 total), and the pass mark is 40 points per subject. The exam is passed when both 無線工学 and 法規 are passed, so questions are grouped into 12-question mock exams. Questions and answer choices are shuffled.
